## 3. Extended window → full pipeline

`prepare --date 2026-11-26 --as-of 2026-11-26T03:30Z --lookback-hours 744 --live-links` (31 days; disclosed in the edition):

**Collection.** 62 discovered, 5 in-window articles retrieved and stored, 49 out of window, and 8 index pages rejected. The articles were the IPO roadshow (10 Nov), pricing (18 Nov) and closing (20 Nov), the GSA agreement (27 Oct) and AI for Science (28 Oct).

**Clustering.** 3 events. The three IPO documents formed one cluster, with independent corroboration = 1 because they share one publisher.

**Verification.** 31 atomic claims: 19 supported, 10 qualified, 2 unverified.
- Both unverified claims are GSA customer anecdotes (the Utah pilot and the state-IT repository mapping). They failed `event_state_supported` because the lexical stage check does not recognise "gave"/"used". The gate held them; nothing was overridden.
- Automatic supersession: the roadshow's 825,000,000-share proposal and 123,750,000-share option were marked superseded by the pricing document's figures.
- 5 claims were qualified by the semantic-support pass. For example, "estimated to save $1 billion" does not say who made the estimate.

**Selection.**
- Accepted: the IPO and private placement (priority 4.2) and the GSA discount (3.25).
- Rejected: AI for Science (1.65, below the 2.0 threshold).
- Result: a short edition, which preflight flags as a warning.

**01 Consequence.**
- The IPO consequence received a **revise** verdict from the challenge pass. Its opening generalised from one listing, and it stated the effect on rivals as an outcome. It was revised once (narrowed and made conditional) and then passed.
- The GSA consequence passed.
- Both carry assumptions, competing explanations and disconfirming indicators.

**02 Position.** Three candidates:
- An "implementation services for government" opportunity substantially restated POS-000001, so it was **routed to the Continuity Ledger** rather than issued as a duplicate. Its internal rubric left urgency, monetisation, saturation and reachable market unknown, so no priority was computed.
- The adversarial review returned "revise" on all three. Among other things, it raised the **conflict of interest**: Anthropic sources, an Anthropic subject, analysed with an Anthropic model.
- Selected: **POS-000002 (MONITOR)**, a caution not to read the $26.0 billion Class N placement as strategic alignment until the share rights are known. Its falsifiable first test is to read the Class N terms in the offering documents.

**Continuity Ledger.**
- POS-000001 was matched by entity and topic. The new GSA claims (published after 2026-10-02) produced a proposal **EMERGING → STRENGTHENING**, referencing V1.
- The proposal states explicitly that V1 is **not** met: no individual organisation committed to organisation-wide coding-agent use, and the figures are self-reported.
- The IPO claims were judged irrelevant to that position.
- The proposal is recorded but not yet a revision. It becomes an append-only revision only when an editor approves the issue.

**Editorial review.**
- The draft adversarial check passed with three minor findings.
- On reviewing that check, a conflict-of-interest disclosure was added to the edition notes (draft v2); every recorded analysis output was reused.
- Preflight, including the **live re-check of all 4 links**: 0 blocking failures. 2 warnings: only 2 signals, and a single-publisher source base.
- Preview PDF: 5 pages, tagged, selectable text, active links to every source.

## Is this edition publishable?

No, and the engine says so. It is a single-publisher, self-reported, extended-window edition about the company whose model produced the analysis. The run demonstrates the pipeline, the gates and the hand-off to a human editor. It is not a specimen for `/brief`. A launch specimen needs independent sources (network access and source adapters) and an independent analysis path (an engine API key, plus editor scrutiny).
